# Blind Paid-Ads Audit: Heavy A4 vs. Thin `13 x 18 cm`

Date: `2026-11-24`.
Three independent CRO and paid-ads auditors (`Argon`, `Carbon`, `Neon`) compared `OLD 13x18` against `NEW A4 21x29.7x3.0 cm`.

## 1. Test Setup and Unit Economics

- **Retail target**: `249 PLN` (`~58 EUR`) for Poland and EU D2C cold traffic on Meta and TikTok Ads.
- **Candidates**: Thin `13 x 18 cm` (`7.5 mm` frame, no mat) vs. heavy `A4 21 x 29.7 x 3.0 cm` white 45-deg beveled passe-partout (`12 x 17 cm` window, no glass).
- **Resin cost parity**: Both formats print the exact same two Mono 4 tiles (`~160 g` resin, same print time).
- **Frame COGS delta**: Heavy A4 adds only `15-20 PLN` while increasing total area by `+168%` (`2.7x`) and depth by `4x`.

## 3. Unanimous `3:0` Verdict

- **Keep `NEW A4 21 x 29.7 x 3.0 cm` white passe-partout** as the sole `249 PLN` single format.
- **Retire `OLD 13 x 18 cm`** because postcard proportions trigger sticker shock above `99-129 PLN` and risk unboxing remorse.
- Heavy A4 re-anchors the product from hobby 3D print to gift-ready architectural wall and desk art.

## 4. Three Mandatory Commercial Guardrails

Every paid ad, product card, and fulfillment box must enforce these three rules:

1. **Raking side light (`Argon`)**: Light every hero shot from the side so crisp shadows separate white buildings from white matboard.
2. **`30-45 deg` 3D hero angles (`Carbon`)**: Never lead ads with `90 deg` flatlays. Show angled 3D relief instead.
3. **Recessed shipping and care (`Neon`)**: Pack open-air spires in a deep recessed Paczkomat insert and publish a short dusting FAQ.

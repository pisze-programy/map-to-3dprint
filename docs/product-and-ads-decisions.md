# Product Format and Paid Ads Decisions

This document records finalized product formats, pricing tiers, and social video ad rules.

## 1. Single Format Decision (`249 PLN`)

We tested three single-unit formats across four independent consumer and sales audits.
Customers place the single frame flat on a desk or hang it on a wall.
Never use stands or easels (podstawki) in product design or marketing renders.

| Candidate Format | Audit Result | Key Findings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Thick `13 x 18 cm` with passe-partout | Rejected (`0:4`) | Triggers `19 PLN` IKEA frame anchor; shrinks visible city area. |
| Round `15 cm` (`2` Mono 4 semicircles) | Runner-up | Strong thumb-stop and water contrast; weaker home-use clarity without a stand. |
| **Thin `13 x 18 cm` without passe-partout (Variant C)** | **Winner (`4:0`, `8.45/10`)** | Full-bleed architectural relief; fits desks and walls cleanly. |

## 2. Single-Format Seam Finishing

Variant C spans two Mono 4 tiles inside one low-profile `13 x 18 cm` frame.
Mask the seam between both tiles with this finishing sequence:

1. Use a building-safe split line when the street grid allows it.
2. Fill the joint with matching white liquid resin.
3. Cure the filled seam under UV light.
4. Apply a matte clear coat across the assembled relief.

## 3. Wall Set Expansion Formats

Scale single-city buyers into larger wall compositions with two collective frame sets:

- **Triptych `3x1`**: Price at `599 PLN` inside one collective `23 x 50 cm` frame.
- **Quartet `2x2`**: Price at `699 PLN` inside one collective `30 x 40 cm` or `40 x 50 cm` frame.

## 4. Meta and TikTok Ad Creative Grammar (`15 s`, `9:16`)

Design every `9:16` Reels, Meta, and TikTok ad with this exact pacing:

1. **Hook (`0-2 s`)**: Show the full recognizable framed city at once. Give instant category clarity and visual curiosity (Kinder ad pacing).
2. **Kinetic sequence (`2-11 s`)**: Keep the camera moving with zero dead freezes. Combine four dynamic moves:
   - Speed-ramp crash-zoom into the tallest skyline peak.
   - Low-angle crane rise-up with sweeping raking side light.
   - Overhead-to-canyon swoop between tall towers.
   - Smooth crane pull-out back to the complete framed desk object.
3. **Scale reveal (`11-15 s`)**: Include a human hand scale reveal so viewers feel physical size and `10K` detail.

## 5. Production Visual Standard

- Render all product stills and ad footage in photorealistic Blender Cycles environments.
- Never publish flat WebGL void captures as paid ad creative.
- Export `9:16` `Start -> End` keyframes for Google Flow (Veo) or render direct Cycles MP4 clips.
- Read `docs/blender-studio-workflow.md` for exact CLI commands and MCP tools.
